Location: Honduras 

 Event: On May 31 the Government of Honduras declared a national health emergency due to a significant increase in dengue fever across the country.  Dengue fever is endemic in Honduras and is considered a year-round, nationwide threat.  The risk is highest from May to October.  The Honduran Ministry of Health, reported that Honduras is currently experiencing a surgein dengue cases. Health officials reported elevated dengue fever activity nationwide, with a total of 20,563 cases reported from January 1 to May 12, compared to the 3,771 cases reported over a similar period in 2023. Health regions reporting elevated disease activity include Metropolitana DC, Cortes, Metropolitana San Pedro Sula, La Paz, and Atlantida. 

 Action to Take: 

  • Visit the Center for Disease Control (CDC) travelers' health page at:  https://www.cdc.gov/  

  • Consult the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention https://www.cdc.gov/ for country health advisories or call CDC at 800-232-4636 or 404-639-3534.
